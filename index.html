How to play

  • Build – pick something from the bar at the bottom (or press 1–8) and click on the meadow.
  • Rotate – R or mouse wheel click while placing.
  • Move – W A S D, or drag with the left mouse when nothing is selected.
  • Orbit – right-drag, or Q / E. Zoom – scroll.
  • Demolish – X then click. You get half the wood back.
  • Esc or right-click to put the tool down. Space pauses.

Villagers find jobs on their own. Woodcutters fell trees, farmers grow wheat, fishers catch fish in the lake. Build cottages so new families can move in – they come faster when the village is cosy.

Your village saves itself automatically.
