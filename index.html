How to play

  • Build – pick something from the bar at the bottom (or press the number keys, L for lanterns) and click on the meadow.
  • Rotate – R or middle-click while placing.
  • Move – drag with the left mouse, or W A S D.
  • Orbit – right-drag, or Q / E. Zoom – scroll.
  • Paths – click and drag to paint. Paint across the brook to build a bridge.
  • Demolish – X then click a building, or drag over paths. You get half the wood back.
  • Villagers – click one to see how they're feeling, rename them, or follow them around.
  • Esc or right-click to put the tool down. Space pauses.
  • Look around – drag with one finger. Pinch to zoom, twist two fingers to turn.
  • Build – pick something from the bar, drag the see-through building (or tap the meadow) to move it, then press Build.
  • Rotate – the ↻ button.
  • Paths – drag one finger to paint, or tap for a single tile. Paint across the brook to build a bridge. Use two fingers to move the view while painting.
  • Demolish – 🧹, then tap a building and confirm, or drag over paths to erase them.
  • Tap a villager to see how they're feeling, rename them, or follow them around. Tap a building to see what it's up to.

Villagers find jobs on their own. Woodcutters fell trees, farmers grow wheat, fishers catch fish, and a baker turns wheat into far more bread. Build cottages so new families can move in – they come faster when the village is cosy.

Keep hens, cows and sheep for eggs, milk and wool – wool keeps everyone cosy through winter. Every villager has a favourite spot they love to wander to.

The seasons turn every few days. Crops grow slowly in winter, so stock up on food in autumn.

Your village saves itself automatically.
